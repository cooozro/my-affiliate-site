    <h2 style="font-size: 22px; font-weight: 700; border-left: 4px solid #000; padding-left: 10px; margin-bottom: 20px;">1. Introduction: The Polarization of the Smartwatch Market</h2>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">Today's wearable tech market is divided into two major camps. On one side, we have high-end flagship lineups from Apple and Samsung, costing anywhere from $200 to $500+, boasting advanced medical sensors and proprietary mobile app ecosystems. They dominate the wrists of premium users with seamless smartphone integration and professional health data analysis.</p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">On the other side, a completely different demand is exploding. These are practical consumers who ask, "Why spend hundreds on a watch?", "I'm tired of the hassle of daily charging," or "I just need notifications, basic health tracking, and a clean design." While the market is flooded with cheap smartwatches, most disappoint with flimsy plastic bodies or washed-out LCD screens.</p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">Amidst this, one product has become a hot topic among global tech enthusiasts: the <strong>TOZO S8 Aura Smartwatch</strong>, armed with a high-strength metal frame, a vivid high-definition AMOLED display, and impressive battery efficiency. The AIPICK editorial team has conducted an in-depth analysis to see what value this product truly offers compared to the market's heavy hitters.</p>

    <h2 style="font-size: 22px; font-weight: 700; border-left: 4px solid #000; padding-left: 10px; margin-bottom: 20px;">3. Deep Dive: Strengths and Limitations</h2>
    <h3 style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 25px; margin-bottom: 10px;">① Design: Metal Elegance Beyond its Price</h3>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">The TOZO S8 Aura stands out with its high-strength metal frame and precise polishing, offering a premium look. Its 1.32-inch AMOLED panel (466x466) provides excellent color and readability even under direct sunlight.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 25px; margin-bottom: 10px;">② Battery Life: Freedom from Charging Stress</h3>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">Unlike flagships that require daily charging, the TOZO S8 Aura lasts up to 10 days on a single charge. This provides immense convenience for travelers and busy professionals.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 25px; margin-bottom: 10px;">③ Health & Smart Features</h3>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">It includes 24/7 heart rate, SpO2, stress monitoring, and 100+ sports modes. While it lacks medical-grade sensors like ECG or blood pressure monitoring found in expensive flagships, it is perfect for daily wellness tracking and notifications.</p>
