    <h2 style="font-size: 22px; font-weight: 700; border-left: 4px solid #000; padding-left: 10px; margin-bottom: 20px;">1. Introduction: Market Polarization and the Rise of Practical Wearables</h2>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">The wearable tech market is currently divided into two major camps. On one side, we have high-end flagship lineups from Apple and Samsung, costing between $200 and $500, boasting advanced medical sensors and proprietary app ecosystems. They dominate the wrists of premium users with seamless smartphone integration and professional health data analysis.</p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">However, a completely different demand is exploding on the other side. These are practical consumers who ask, "Do I really need to spend hundreds of dollars on a watch?", "I'm tired of the hassle of charging every night," or "I just need notifications, basic health tracking, and a clean design." While the market is flooded with low-cost smartwatches, most disappoint with cheap plastic bodies or washed-out LCD screens.</p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">In this climate, one product has recently become a hot topic among global tech enthusiasts: the <strong>TOZO S8 Aura Smartwatch</strong>, armed with a high-strength metal frame, a vivid high-definition AMOLED display, and impressive battery efficiency. The AIPICK editorial team has analyzed whether this product provides real value compared to the market's heavyweights.</p>

    <h2 style="font-size: 22px; font-weight: 700; border-left: 4px solid #000; padding-left: 10px; margin-bottom: 20px;">3. In-Depth Analysis: Strengths and Limitations</h2>
    <h3 style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 25px; margin-bottom: 10px;">① Display and Design: Metal Elegance Beyond its Price</h3>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">The TOZO S8 Aura features a high-strength metal frame and a 1.32-inch AMOLED panel (466x466 resolution), offering excellent clarity even under direct sunlight. While it lacks the curved glass of an Apple Watch, it provides top-tier visual satisfaction for its price point.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 25px; margin-bottom: 10px;">② Battery Life: Freedom from Charging Stress</h3>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">Unlike flagship watches that require daily charging, the TOZO S8 Aura offers up to 10 days of battery life in typical use and up to 30 days in standby. This provides immense convenience for travelers and busy professionals.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 25px; margin-bottom: 10px;">③ Health and Smart Features</h3>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">It includes essential sensors for heart rate, SpO2, stress, and sleep tracking, plus 100+ sports modes. While it lacks medical-grade features like ECG or blood pressure monitoring found in Apple/Samsung flagships, it is more than sufficient for daily wellness and notification management.</p>
