    <h2 style="font-size: 22px; font-weight: 700; border-left: 4px solid #000; padding-left: 10px; margin-bottom: 20px;">1. Introduction: The Polarization of the Smartwatch Market</h2>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">The wearable tech market is currently split into two major camps. On one side, we have high-end flagship lineups from Apple and Samsung, costing between $200 and $500, boasting advanced medical sensors and proprietary app ecosystems. They dominate the wrists of premium users with seamless smartphone integration and professional health data analysis.</p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">However, a completely different demand is exploding on the other side. These are practical consumers who ask, "Do I really need to spend hundreds of dollars on a watch?", "I'm tired of charging every night," or "I just need notifications, basic health tracking, and a clean design." While the market is flooded with cheap smartwatches, most disappoint with flimsy plastic bodies or washed-out LCD screens.</p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">In this climate, one product has become a hot topic among global tech enthusiasts: the <strong>TOZO S8 Aura Smartwatch</strong>, featuring a high-strength metal frame, a vivid AMOLED display, and incredible battery efficiency. The AIPICK editorial team has analyzed this device to see if it truly delivers value compared to the heavyweights.</p>

    <h2 style="font-size: 22px; font-weight: 700; border-left: 4px solid #000; padding-left: 10px; margin-bottom: 20px;">3. Deep Dive: Strengths and Limitations</h2>
    <h3 style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 25px; margin-bottom: 10px;">① Design: Metal Elegance</h3>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">The TOZO S8 Aura stands out with its high-strength metal frame and 1.32-inch AMOLED display (353 PPI). It offers excellent visibility even in direct sunlight, punching well above its price point.</p>
    <h3 style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 25px; margin-bottom: 10px;">② Battery Life</h3>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">Unlike Apple or Samsung watches that require daily charging, the S8 Aura lasts up to 10 days on a single charge, providing significant convenience for travelers and busy professionals.</p>
    <h3 style="font-size: 18px; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 25px; margin-bottom: 10px;">③ Health & Smart Features</h3>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 15px;">It covers all essentials: 24/7 heart rate, SpO2, stress tracking, and Bluetooth calling. While it lacks medical-grade ECG or blood pressure monitoring, it is perfect for daily wellness and notification management.</p>
