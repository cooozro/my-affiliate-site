  <section class="comparison-section">
    <h3>2. Key Comparison Points: What's the Difference?</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Massive Difference in Battery Life:</strong> While Apple Watch and Galaxy Watch require daily charging, the TOZO S8 Aura offers up to 10 days of battery life, completely eliminating battery anxiety.</li>
      <li><strong>Value Meets Premium Design:</strong> Despite its budget-friendly price point around $50–$80, it features a durable metal frame and a crisp AMOLED display, avoiding the cheap "toy-like" feel common in entry-level wearables.</li>
      <li><strong>Medical-Grade Features vs. Everyday Use:</strong> Galaxy and Apple Watches command premium prices because they offer medical-grade ECG and body composition tracking. However, if your main goals are checking notifications, taking calls, and tracking basic workouts and sleep, the TOZO S8 Aura offers far superior value for money.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section class="analysis-section">
    <h3>3. AIPICK Final Recommendation Guide</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Buy the TOZO S8 Aura if:</strong> You hate daily charging and want a crisp AMOLED screen, sleek metal design, and Bluetooth calling on a budget under $100.</li>
      <li><strong>Buy the Apple/Galaxy Watch if:</strong> You need seamless integration with your phone's app ecosystem, mobile payment features, and precision medical-grade health monitoring.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
