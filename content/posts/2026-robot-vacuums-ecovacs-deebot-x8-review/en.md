## What it costs to keep, not just to buy

When you look at the Ecovacs DEEBOT X8 PRO OMNI, the first number that jumps out is the purchase price. But for anyone living in an apartment, the real question is what this robot vacuum costs to keep running over three years. The OMNI station is the centerpiece—it auto-empties the dustbin, washes and dries the mop pads, and refills the water tank. That convenience comes with consumables you will need to replace: dust bags, mop pads, filters, and cleaning solution. Over 36 months, we estimate the total cost of ownership lands around **$1,100 to $1,300** when you factor in the initial price and parts. That is roughly **{{krw:29.99}}** per month in consumables alone, which is modest compared to the time you save.

**Editorial read:** The X8 PRO OMNI is not the cheapest robot vacuum to run, but the maintenance burden is far lower than a model that forces you to empty the bin every few days. For an apartment dweller who values convenience, the extra cost is often worth it.

## How it behaves in real rooms

![Ecovacs DEEBOT X8 PRO OMNI in a real-world lifestyle setting (stock photo illustration)](/images/posts/2026-robot-vacuums-ecovacs-deebot-x8-review/body-1-c03388.jpg)

*Staged category example image (not a photo of the actual product). Photo by Jens Mahnke / Pexels*

In a typical apartment—a mix of hardwood, tile, and a few area rugs—the X8 PRO OMNI does a solid job. The LiDAR mapping is quick; it builds a floor plan in about 10 minutes for a 700-square-foot space. You can set no-go zones and virtual walls from the app, which is essential if you have pet bowls or a tangle of cables.

Suction-wise, the 18,000 Pa is overkill for bare floors but makes a noticeable difference on rugs. It pulls up pet hair and fine dust that a 6,000 Pa model would leave behind. The mop pads lift automatically when transitioning to carpet, so you do not get wet streaks on rugs. On tile, the dual spinning pads scrub with decent pressure, but they are not a substitute for a manual mop on sticky spills—you will still want to spot-clean those.

**Review concern:** The OMNI station takes up a fair amount of floor space—roughly 16 x 16 inches—so you need a dedicated spot near a power outlet. In a cramped apartment, that can be a dealbreaker.

## Strengths and weaknesses

### Strengths
- **Excellent suction** for a robot vacuum, especially on rugs.
- **True hands-free maintenance** with auto-empty, mop washing, and drying.
- **Reliable mapping** with easy app controls and no-go zones.
- **Great for pet owners**—picks up hair without tangling the brush (self-cleaning brush roll).

### Weaknesses
- **High upfront cost** compared to mid-range models.
- **Bulky station** requires floor space.
- **Mop performance is not enough** for deep-cleaning sticky messes.
- **Consumables add up** over time (dust bags, mop pads, filters, solution).

**Analysis takeaway:** For apartments, the X8 PRO OMNI shines if you prioritize convenience and have the budget. If you rarely mop and have mostly bare floors, a cheaper model with auto-empty might be enough.

## FAQ

### How often do I need to replace the dust bag in the OMNI station?

With normal use in a one-bedroom apartment, the dust bag lasts about 60 to 90 days. If you have pets, you may need to change it every month. The app tracks the bag usage and sends a notification, so you don't have to guess.

### Can I control the X8 PRO OMNI with voice assistants?

Yes, it works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. You can say “Alexa, ask Ecovacs to clean the living room” or use the Google Home app to start a cleaning cycle. The app also supports scheduling and zone cleaning.

### Is the mop function good enough to replace manual mopping?

For everyday maintenance, yes—it keeps tile and hardwood floors clean. But for sticky spills or heavy grime, you'll still need a manual mop. The robot's mop pads are more for light cleaning and dust removal.

### How loud is the X8 PRO OMNI during operation?

In standard mode, it's around 67 dB, which is comparable to a conversation. The auto-empty station is louder, briefly hitting about 75 dB when it sucks the dust into the bag. You might want to run it when you're not in the room.

### What maintenance do I need to do myself?

You'll need to replace the dust bag every 1-3 months, clean the filter monthly, and replace the mop pads every 3-6 months. The station's water tank needs refilling, and you should clean the washboard periodically to prevent buildup. The app provides maintenance reminders.

## Final Verdict
The Ecovacs DEEBOT X8 PRO OMNI is one of the most complete robot vacuums for apartment living in 2026. It combines powerful suction, reliable mapping, and a station that handles most maintenance automatically. The main drawbacks are the price and the station's footprint. If you have a small space with bare floors and a tight budget, you can get away with a simpler model. But if you want to set it and forget it, and you have rugs or pets, the X8 PRO OMNI is a strong buy.

**Who should buy:** Apartment dwellers with a mix of flooring, pet owners, and anyone who hates emptying dust bins.

**Who should skip:** Budget shoppers, people with very small spaces lacking outlet space, and those who expect robot mopping to replace manual mopping entirely.

**Buy/wait/skip:** Buy if you value hands-free maintenance and have the budget. Wait if you can find a deal on the previous generation. Skip if you rarely mop and want to save money.
