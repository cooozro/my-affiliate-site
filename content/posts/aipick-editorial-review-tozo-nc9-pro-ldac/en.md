      2. Sound Quality Verification: LDAC Codec and 10mm SDLC Driver
    </h2>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
      The TOZO NC9 Pro supports the high-resolution <strong>LDAC</strong> codec and features a 10mm SDLC dynamic driver with TOZO OrigX Pro acoustic tuning. LDAC allows for higher data transmission compared to standard SBC/AAC codecs on supported devices (primarily Android), which helps reduce audio loss. However, it is important to note that <strong>iPhones do not support LDAC</strong>; iOS users will connect via the AAC codec, meaning the benefits of LDAC will not be noticeable.
    </p>
    <ul style="padding-left: 1.5rem; background: rgba(128, 128, 128, 0.06); border: 1px solid rgba(128, 128, 128, 0.2); padding: 1.2rem 1.2rem 1.2rem 2.5rem; border-radius: 8px;">

      3. Noise Cancellation (ANC) and Call Quality: Performance by the Numbers
    </h2><p style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
      The TOZO NC9 Pro features hybrid ANC technology and, according to official TOZO data, supports <strong>up to 45dB of noise reduction</strong>. Compared to the base model (NC9), it adds <strong>Adaptive ANC</strong>, which automatically adjusts the noise-canceling intensity based on your surroundings. In terms of call quality, it supports a 6-mic ENC (Environmental Noise Cancellation) system, allowing for relatively clear voice transmission even in environments with external noise.
    </p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
      However, the 45dB figure is based on the manufacturer's testing environment (typically measured against white noise in specific frequency bands), so the perceived reduction may vary with broadband or irregular noise like subway sounds or wind. Additionally, independent measurements by external review outlets (such as SoundGuys) have occasionally shown that the ANC duration for TOZO's entry-level lineup is slightly shorter than the manufacturer's official figures. As with battery life, it is best to keep in mind that official specs are based on "optimal conditions."
    </p>

    <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 700; border-bottom: 2px solid rgba(191, 219, 254, 0.4); padding-bottom: 0.5rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

    <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem; margin-top:1.4rem;">Can I use LDAC on the TOZO NC9 Pro with an iPhone?</h3>
    <p>No. LDAC is a codec developed by Sony, and it is not officially supported by iOS. iPhones will automatically connect using the AAC codec, in which case the high-quality transmission benefits of LDAC are difficult to experience.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem; margin-top:1.4rem;">Does the case display significantly reduce battery life?</h3>
    <p>TOZO has not officially disclosed the specific battery capacity of the NC9 Pro's case, so exact figures are unknown. However, because it features an always-on display, it is highly likely that the total usage time will be slightly lower than the base NC9 (up to 59 hours), which lacks a display.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem; margin-top:1.4rem;">What is the difference between Hybrid ANC and Adaptive ANC?</h3>
    <p>Hybrid ANC is a standard noise-canceling method that uses microphones both inside and outside the earbuds to cancel out noise. Adaptive ANC is an advanced feature added to the NC9 Pro that goes a step further by detecting ambient noise levels in real-time and automatically adjusting the ANC intensity.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem; margin-top:1.4rem;">Should I buy the TOZO NC9 Pro or the base NC9?</h3>
    <p>If you are an Android user who wants LDAC high-quality audio and a smart display UX, the Pro is the way to go. If you prioritize battery life relative to price, the base NC9 may be the more rational choice.</p>
