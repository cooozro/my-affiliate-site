Unlike traditional wireless earbuds that only indicate battery levels through LED blinks or app connectivity, you can directly check and control settings like language, connection status, battery life, and EQ presets right from the case screen. On the unit we received, the time and battery level were clearly visible on the display, and it remained legible even when not plugged into power.

While this is an attractive feature for those who love smart functionality, it is a point to consider for those who prioritize "maximum battery longevity" above all else.

    <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 700; border-bottom: 2px solid rgba(191, 219, 254, 0.4); padding-bottom: 0.5rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
      2. Sound Verification: LDAC Codec and 10mm SDLC Driver
    </h2>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
      The TOZO NC9 Pro supports <strong>LDAC</strong>, a high-resolution codec, and features a 10mm SDLC dynamic driver with TOZO OrigX Pro acoustic tuning. The packaging displays Hi-Res Audio Wireless and LDAC certification marks. LDAC allows supported devices (primarily Android) to transmit more audio data compared to standard SBC/AAC codecs, which helps reduce audio loss. However, it is important to note that <strong>iPhones do not support LDAC</strong>; iOS users will connect via the AAC codec, meaning they won't be able to experience the benefits of LDAC.
    </p>
    <ul style="padding-left: 1.5rem; background: rgba(128, 128, 128, 0.06); border: 1px solid rgba(128, 128, 128, 0.2); padding: 1.2rem 1.2rem 1.2rem 2.5rem; border-radius: 8px;">
      <li style="margin-bottom: 0.8rem;"><strong>Resolution:</strong> The muffled sound often found in budget earphones is less noticeable here, and you can definitely feel the detail in the mid-to-high frequencies, especially when LDAC is active.</li>
      <li><strong>Bass Tuning:</strong> The bass is relatively stable and doesn't overly emphasize punchiness, making it comfortable for long listening sessions.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

    <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 700; border-bottom: 2px solid rgba(191, 219, 254, 0.4); padding-bottom: 0.5rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">
      3. Noise Cancellation (ANC) and Call Quality: Performance by the Numbers
    </h2>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
      The TOZO NC9 Pro is equipped with hybrid ANC technology and supports <strong>up to 45dB of noise reduction</strong> according to official TOZO data. Compared to the base model (NC9), it adds <strong>Adaptive ANC</strong>, which automatically adjusts the noise cancellation intensity based on your surroundings. For call quality, it supports a 6-mic ENC (Environmental Noise Cancellation) system, allowing for relatively clear voice transmission even in noisy environments.
    </p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
      However, keep in mind that the 45dB figure is based on the manufacturer's testing environment (usually measured against white noise in specific frequency bands). The actual reduction you experience may vary with broadband or irregular noises like subway sounds or wind. It is best to view official specs as "optimal condition" results.
    </p>
  </section>

    <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 700; border-bottom: 2px solid rgba(191, 219, 254, 0.4); padding-bottom: 0.5rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

    <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem; margin-top:1.4rem;">Can I use LDAC on the TOZO NC9 Pro with an iPhone?</h3>
    <p>No. LDAC is a codec developed by Sony and is not officially supported by iOS. iPhones will automatically connect using the AAC codec, in which case the high-fidelity benefits of LDAC are difficult to perceive.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem; margin-top:1.4rem;">Does the case display drain the battery significantly?</h3>
    <p>Since TOZO has not officially disclosed the battery capacity of the NC9 Pro case alone, exact figures are unknown. However, because it features an always-on display, it is highly likely that the total usage time will be slightly lower than the base NC9 (up to 59 hours), which lacks a display.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem; margin-top:1.4rem;">What is the difference between Hybrid ANC and Adaptive ANC?</h3>
    <p>Hybrid ANC is a standard noise-canceling method that uses microphones both inside and outside the earbuds to cancel out noise. Adaptive ANC is an advanced feature added to the NC9 Pro that goes a step further by detecting ambient noise in real-time and automatically adjusting the intensity of the ANC.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem; margin-top:1.4rem;">Should I buy the TOZO NC9 Pro or the base NC9?</h3>
    <p>If you are an Android user who wants LDAC high-fidelity audio and the smart display UX, the Pro is the way to go. If you prioritize battery life over price, the base NC9 may be the more rational choice.</p>
