      2. Sound Quality: LDAC Codec and 10mm SDLC Driver
    </h2>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
      The TOZO NC9 Pro supports the high-resolution <strong>LDAC</strong> codec and features a 10mm SDLC dynamic driver with TOZO OrigX Pro sound tuning. LDAC allows for higher audio data transmission compared to standard SBC/AAC codecs on supported devices (primarily Android), which helps reduce audio loss. However, it is important to note that <strong>iPhones do not support LDAC</strong>; iOS users will connect via the AAC codec, making it difficult to fully experience the benefits of LDAC.
    </p>
    <ul style="padding-left: 1.5rem; background: rgba(128, 128, 128, 0.06); border: 1px solid rgba(128, 128, 128, 0.2); padding: 1.2rem 1.2rem 1.2rem 2.5rem; border-radius: 8px;">
      <li style="margin-bottom: 0.8rem;"><strong>Resolution:</strong> The muffled sound often found in budget earbuds is less noticeable here, and you can feel the detail in the mid-to-high frequencies, especially when LDAC is active.</li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 0.8rem;"><strong>Bass Tuning:</strong> The bass is relatively stable without over-emphasizing the punch, making it comfortable for long listening sessions.</li>
      <li><strong>Limitations:</strong> At the time of this review, there is no official confirmation in the public certification list that TOZO has obtained specific international Hi-Res certification marks. Please be aware that the claim "capable of Hi-Res quality audio transmission" is different from being a "Hi-Res certified product."</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

      3. Noise Cancellation (ANC) and Call Quality: Performance by the Numbers
    </h2>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
      The TOZO NC9 Pro is equipped with hybrid ANC technology and, according to official TOZO data, supports <strong>up to 45dB of noise reduction</strong>. Additionally, it features <strong>Adaptive ANC</strong>, which automatically adjusts the noise cancellation intensity based on the surrounding environment—a feature not present in the base model (NC9). In terms of call quality, the 6-mic ENC (Environmental Noise Cancellation) system allows for relatively clear voice transmission even in noisy environments.
    </p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
      However, the 45dB figure is based on the manufacturer's testing environment (typically using white noise in specific frequency bands), so the actual reduction may feel different when dealing with broadband or irregular noises like subway sounds or wind. Furthermore, independent measurements by external review outlets (such as SoundGuys) have occasionally shown that the ANC performance of TOZO's entry-level lineup is shorter than the official manufacturer specs. As with the battery life, it is best to keep in mind that official specifications are based on "optimal conditions."
    </p>

    <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 700; border-bottom: 2px solid rgba(191, 219, 254, 0.4); padding-bottom: 0.5rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

    <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem; margin-top:1.4rem;">Can I use LDAC on the TOZO NC9 Pro with an iPhone?</h3>
    <p>No. LDAC is a codec developed by Sony that is not officially supported by iOS. iPhones will automatically connect using the AAC codec, which does not provide the high-fidelity benefits of LDAC.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem; margin-top:1.4rem;">Does the case display significantly reduce battery life?</h3>
    <p>TOZO has not officially disclosed the specific battery capacity of the NC9 Pro case, so exact figures are unknown. However, because the design includes an always-on display, it is likely that the total usage time will be slightly lower than the base NC9 (up to 59 hours), which lacks a display.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem; margin-top:1.4rem;">What is the difference between Hybrid ANC and Adaptive ANC?</h3>
    <p>Hybrid ANC is the standard noise-canceling method that uses microphones both inside and outside the earbuds to cancel out noise. Adaptive ANC is an advanced feature added to the NC9 Pro that detects ambient noise in real-time and automatically adjusts the intensity of the ANC.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem; margin-top:1.4rem;">Should I buy the TOZO NC9 Pro or the base NC9?</h3>
    <p>If you are an Android user who wants LDAC high-quality audio and the smart display UX, the Pro is the better choice. If you prioritize battery life for the price, the base NC9 may be the more rational option.</p>
