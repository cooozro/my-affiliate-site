    </h2>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">

LDAC allows for higher data transmission compared to standard SBC/AAC codecs on supported devices (primarily Android), which helps reduce audio loss. However, it is important to note that <strong>iPhones do not support LDAC</strong>; iOS users will be connected via the AAC codec, making it difficult to fully experience the benefits of LDAC.
    </p>
    <ul style="padding-left: 1.5rem; background: rgba(128, 128, 128, 0.06); border: 1px solid rgba(128, 128, 128, 0.2); padding: 1.2rem 1.2rem 1.2rem 2.5rem; border-radius: 8px;">

      3. Noise Cancellation (ANC) and Call Quality: Performance by the Numbers
    </h2><p style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">

Compared to the base model (NC9), it adds <strong>Adaptive ANC</strong>, which automatically adjusts the noise-canceling intensity based on your surrounding environment. In terms of call quality, it supports a 6-mic ENC (Environmental Noise Cancellation) system, allowing for relatively clear voice transmission even in noisy outdoor settings.
    </p>
    <p style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
      However, the 45dB figure is based on the manufacturer's testing environment (typically measured against white noise in specific frequency bands), so the actual reduction you experience with broadband or irregular noise, such as subway sounds or wind, may vary. Additionally, independent measurements by external review outlets (like SoundGuys) have occasionally shown that the ANC duration for TOZO's entry-level lineup is slightly shorter than the official manufacturer figures. As with battery life, it is best to keep in mind that official specs are based on "optimal conditions."

    <h2 style="font-size: 1.5rem; font-weight: 700; border-bottom: 2px solid rgba(191, 219, 254, 0.4); padding-bottom: 0.5rem; margin-bottom: 1.2rem;">Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

    <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem; margin-top:1.4rem;">Can I use LDAC on the TOZO NC9 Pro with an iPhone?</h3>
    <p>No. LDAC is a codec developed by Sony that is not officially supported by iOS. iPhones will automatically connect using the AAC codec, which makes it difficult to experience the high-quality transmission benefits of LDAC.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem; margin-top:1.4rem;">Does the case display significantly reduce battery life?</h3>
    <p>TOZO has not officially disclosed the battery capacity of the NC9 Pro case alone, so exact figures are unknown. However, because the design includes an always-on display, it is highly likely that the total usage time will be slightly lower than the base NC9 (up to 59 hours), which lacks a display.</p>

    <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem; margin-top:1.4rem;">What is the difference between Hybrid ANC and Adaptive ANC?</h3>
    <p>Hybrid ANC is a standard noise-canceling method that uses microphones both inside and outside the earbuds to cancel out noise. Adaptive ANC is an advanced feature added to the NC9 Pro that goes a step further by detecting ambient noise in real-time and automatically adjusting the intensity of the ANC.</p>
